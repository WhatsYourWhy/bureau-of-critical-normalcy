05 / Questions nobody needed answered

Frequently overreacted.

Is this a real cybersecurity organization?+

No. The Bureau is fictional. This site is cybersecurity satire about inflated severity scores, product branding, attention economics, and the thrill of discovering that a button performs its stated function.

Are any of these real CVEs or advisories?+

No. BCN numbers are fictional internal joke identifiers, not official CVE IDs. No real vulnerabilities are alleged, and the absurd scores are deliberately outside real scoring limits.

Does the threat scanner access my computer?+

No. NothingScan is a local animation. It does not run security tests, contact a server, collect device data, or inspect files. It is remarkably effective at discovering nothing.

Can I trust your research?+

You can trust that we are deeply committed to taking obvious things too seriously. For real security advice, consult credible technical reports and verified vendor disclosures.

What should I do if my keyboard works?+

Remain calm. We have reason to believe the issue could be widespread. Further typing may reproduce it.
